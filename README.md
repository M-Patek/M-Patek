## 🧭 Research Method

What I do across projects:

### Problem Decomposition

I reduce broad objectives to the smallest tractable units, separating genuine frontier difficulty from noise, confusion, and decorative complexity.

### Research Steering

When a line of attack stops paying off, I change frames, borrow from adjacent formalisms, and redirect the search instead of overfitting to a dead path.

### Meta-Reasoning

I care about epistemic discipline: whether claims have drifted, whether constraints remain intact, and whether the system is still solving the problem it originally set out to solve.

### AI Orchestration

I use AI inside a structured loop. I define goals, boundaries, selection criteria, and correction signals; AI expands, probes, rewrites, and compares candidate paths.
