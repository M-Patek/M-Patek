## The Work

My current work starts from a simple research intuition: AI should not only be studied as a system that produces answers, but as a system whose process of searching, correcting, remembering, constraining, and acting can itself be designed.

I am working around AI-related research, cognitive architecture, agent systems, AI-native research workflows, and Embodied AI.

What interests me most is the transition from response generation to structured cognition: systems that can hold intermediate states, revise their own path, remain close to implementation, and eventually connect abstract reasoning with perception, action, environment, and embodiment.

What I can provide is direction, structure, research sensitivity, and an active orientation toward AI and Embodied AI. I am interested in turning ambiguous problems into executable forms, identifying real bottlenecks, designing feedback loops, and keeping research close enough to experiments that it can be tested.

I am looking for people who are internally driven by research.

The resources I value most are genuine curiosity, long-term attention, technical patience, the ability to read or reproduce papers, the willingness to build and test prototypes, and practical commitment to real experiments through time, compute, hardware, tools, or financial resources.

You do not need to be polished.

But you should be serious enough to stay with a difficult problem until it begins to reveal its real shape.
